# Vietnam reward-item prices (VND, Oct 2026) + pocket-money norms

Context: Vietnamese family in HCMC/Hanoi, two daughters aged 8 and 11. Prices are retail as found Sep–Oct 2026 (some 2025 sources where no newer data). "k" = 1,000 VND, "M" = 1,000,000 VND.

Confidence tags: **[V]** price read directly from a retailer/official page or dated article in this session; **[E]** estimate triangulated from partial sources plus market knowledge (verify before hard-coding).

## Key takeaways

- The price ladder spans roughly 5 orders of magnitude, from a sticker (~20k) to a Singapore trip (~50M). The current categories line up with real prices: small items are ≤100k, medium 100k–1M, large 1M–5M, and dream items 2M–50M+.
- **Bicycle is mis-categorised as "dream".** A good 20" girls' bike costs ~2.2M, about the same as a kids' smartwatch (categorised "large"). Recommend moving it to "large".
- **Height rule for tickets.** VN theme parks and cinemas price "children" by height (<1.4m for parks, often <1.3m for cinemas), not age. An 8-year-old girl (~1.27m) pays the child price. An 11-year-old girl (WHO median ~1.44m) will often pay the **adult** price. Budget accordingly.
- **Apple devices got more expensive in 2026.** iPad A16 is now ~12M (launched under 10M in 2025). Apple Watch for a child without her own iPhone needs Family Setup, which requires a **GPS + Cellular** model (pricier than the 6.39M GPS entry price).

## Pocket money (tiền tiêu vặt): ages 8 and 11, urban HCMC/Hanoi

**Evidence quality is weak.** No 2025–2026 Vietnamese survey publishes pocket money by age. The only survey figures found (70% of parents giving 2–5k/day at primary level and 5–10k/day at lower secondary) date from the early 2010s and are outdated. The 2024 Mastercard Gen Alpha study covers account access only, not amounts. The figures below combine dated parent anecdotes with current prices.

| Age | Common urban practice | Weekly | Monthly | Notes |
|-----|-----------------------|--------|---------|-------|
| 8 (lớp 3) | Many parents give no regular allowance; breakfast/snack money is paid separately (~20k/day). Where an allowance exists, it is small and weekly. | 20–50k (typ. **30k**) | 100–200k | One cited parent: 20k/day breakfast **plus** 50k/week for spending or saving. Articles recommend starting at 7–8 and giving it weekly, not daily. |
| 11 (lớp 6, starting THCS) | Spending needs rise (snacks with friends, class fund, small gifts). Weekly allowance is the common advice. | 50–100k (typ. **70k**) | 200–400k | For an upper bound: an HCMC high-school student got 300k/week and called it insufficient. That is the 15–17 tier, not 11. |
| 13–18 (reference) | Teens | 100–300k | 400k–1.2M | Same SGGP anecdote; amounts vary widely with family income. |

**Calibration for coins.** If an 8-year-old's "fair weekly" earning is ~30k and an 11-year-old's is ~70k:
- A small reward (≤50k) takes about 1 week of earnings.
- A medium reward (~200–500k) takes 1–2 months for the 11-year-old and 2–4 months for the 8-year-old.
- A large item (~2M) takes 6–7 months for the 11-year-old.
- Dream items (12M+) are only reachable by saving together or with parent matching.

Use one coin rate for both girls and scale *earning opportunities* by age, not prices. Otherwise the shared reward catalogue breaks.

## Category fit (suggested adjustments)

- Item 24 (bicycle, 2.2M): move from **dream** to **large**. It costs the same as the kids' smartwatch (2.0M).
- Item 22 (headphones, ~0.9M) sits at the low edge of **large**. It is cheaper than item 20 (large LEGO, 2.2M), which is also in "large", so the category covers roughly 0.9M–4M.
- Item 13 (cinema, family ~520k) and item 16 (restaurant, ~750k) are correctly **medium**, but they are 3–5× item 9 (bubble tea, 50k). Consider splitting "medium" into ~100–300k and ~300k–1M bands.
- Items 26–29 span 6M–50M. "Singapore" is ~4× an iPad, so if coins scale linearly with VND it needs a much higher cost than the other dream items.

## Limitations

- Several major VN retail sites blocked automated fetches: Fahasa, Canifa, Bách Hóa Xanh, Highlands official, Phúc Long, and the TGDĐ category pages. Book, art-supply, Canifa, Phúc Long/Gong Cha, and JBL Kids prices are therefore **[E]**, triangulated from secondary sources.
- Retail prices in VN change weekly with flash sales (MyKingdom and Điện Máy Xanh discounts of 20–50% are common). Treat "typical" as the non-sale street price where the two differ.
- Travel prices (items 21, 28, 29) are highly seasonal. Tết, 30/4, and summer can add 30–60%.
- Pocket-money norms have no current quantitative VN survey. The table above is a reasoned estimate, not measured data.

## Unresolved questions

1. Should reward costs track VND linearly, or should dream items be compressed (e.g. log-scaled) so they stay reachable for an 8-year-old?
2. Is the family in HCMC or Hanoi? This affects the day-trip and theme-park examples (Đầm Sen/Suối Tiên vs. Hồ Tây/Thiên đường Bảo Sơn).
3. Does the 11-year-old already exceed 1.4m? If so, budget adult tickets for cinema and theme parks.
